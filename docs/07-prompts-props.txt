PROMPTS PARA PROPS DEL MUNDO QUE FALTAN (cajón, fogón, cueva, jarilla)
================================================================================

Estilo de referencia
---------------------
Estos NO van en el estilo funko/pixel de los personajes (docs/06-prompts-sprites.txt).
Van en el mismo estilo que ya tienen los props reales que existen hoy en
src/assets/props/ (guanaco.png, coiron.png, manantial.png, restos_costa.png,
bote_menor.png): ilustración pintada en miniatura, vista 3/4 top-down, sombreado
suave, paleta apagada/histórica (tierra, óxido, gris piedra — nada saturado),
sin líneas de contorno duras tipo cómic, objeto aislado sobre fondo transparente.

Cada uno es UN solo objeto estático (no hay direcciones ni poses — a diferencia
de los personajes, estos no caminan). Resolución de trabajo generosa (el juego
los reduce solo, como ya hace con el guanaco/manantial/etc.) — no hace falta
pixel-perfect, con que el objeto quede bien aislado y centrado alcanza.

Cómo usar: pegá el prompt del objeto, sin adjuntar ninguna referencia (no hay
retrato para estos, a diferencia de los personajes). Guardá el resultado con el
nombre de archivo indicado en cada uno y pasámelo — yo lo dejo en
src/assets/props/ y el juego lo levanta solo (ya está cableado para usarlo en
cuanto el archivo exista, no hace falta ningún otro paso de código).

================================================================================
1. CAJÓN — archivo: cajon.png
================================================================================
Small painted illustration of a single weathered wooden shipping crate, as
carried off a 19th-century emigrant ship (1865). Rough-sawn wood planks,
visible grain and weathering, simple rope or thin metal banding holding it
together, a few nail heads. Viewed from a 3/4 top-down angle (like an object
sitting on the ground, seen from slightly above). Soft painterly shading,
muted earthy palette (weathered brown wood, faded rope tan) — no bright or
saturated colors. Isolated object, transparent background, no cast shadow
baked in (the game adds its own).

================================================================================
2. FOGÓN (marca de mapa, de día) — archivo: fogon.png
================================================================================
Small painted illustration of a simple campfire pit, prepared but NOT lit —
daytime, cold fire: a ring of rough stones with a small stack of cut firewood
arranged inside, ready to be lit at night. No flame, no smoke, no embers. Viewed
from a 3/4 top-down angle (like an object sitting on the ground, seen from
slightly above). Soft painterly shading, muted earthy palette (grey stone,
weathered brown wood) — no bright or saturated colors. Isolated object,
transparent background, no cast shadow baked in.
Nota de contexto: esto es el marcador chico que se ve caminando de día por el
mapa — la fogata GRANDE y encendida de noche ya existe como ilustración aparte
(fogon_chico.png / fogon_grande.png, pantalla completa), no se confunde con
esto.

================================================================================
3. CUEVA (boca de cueva) — archivo: cueva.png
================================================================================
Small painted illustration of a single cave entrance dug into a dry clay/earth
embankment (NOT a natural rock cave — it's a shallow dwelling dug by hand into
a soft clay cliff, Patagonian coastal badland). A rough, roughly arched opening,
dark interior (can't see inside), eroded dirt/clay texture around the opening
matching a dry riverbank or ravine wall. Viewed from a 3/4 top-down / slightly
elevated angle, as if seen walking past it on a map. Soft painterly shading,
muted earthy palette (ochre, dark clay, brown) — no bright or saturated colors.
Isolated object, transparent background, no cast shadow baked in.
Nota: un solo diseño alcanza — se reutiliza para las 3 cuevas del campamento
(no hace falta una versión distinta por cueva).

================================================================================
4. JARILLA (arbusto de leña) — archivo: jarilla.png
================================================================================
Small painted illustration of a single "jarilla" bush (creosote bush) — a small,
sparse, wiry Patagonian desert shrub: thin grey-green woody branches, small
sparse leaves, low and scrubby, growing straight out of dry sandy/rocky ground.
Not lush or leafy — dry, hardy, desert scrubland plant. Viewed from a 3/4
top-down angle (like a plant sitting on the ground, seen from slightly above).
Soft painterly shading, muted desaturated palette (grey-green, dusty olive) —
no bright or saturated colors. Isolated object, transparent background, no
cast shadow baked in.
